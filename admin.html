White Pack Admin

Product management · Secure sign-in · Live catalogue updates

Add this private admin tool to your home screen or desktop.

🔐 Supabase Admin Sign In

Sign in with the email and password for the authorized White Pack admin account. Only authenticated users listed in wp_product_admins can save catalogue changes.

📤 Upload Products & Images

Choose several product photos from your phone or computer. Review the product names, categories and prices before saving them to White Pack Supabase. Existing products are not replaced.

📲 Order WhatsApp Number

Change the phone number that receives customer orders. Use the international country code, numbers only (example: 96181085811). This setting is saved to Supabase and applies to the public catalogue after refresh.
📷 Tap here to choose a product photo
Photos are automatically optimized to sharp WebP when supported (up to 1200 × 1200 px) to keep the catalogue clear and fast.
For older products, the existing price is kept as the first price. Category cover tip: if the first product in a category has no price, its photo appears on the main category screen only and it is hidden from the products inside that category.

📂 Category Order

Use the arrows to change category order, or tap Rename to change a category name for every product in that category at once. Changes save to Supabase automatically.

Products 0

Designed for up to 1,000 products. Edit unit labels and prices directly in each row. Tap 📷 Change photo under any product image to replace it immediately—no need to press Edit. Changes save to Supabase automatically.
Fast reorder: enter the product’s new position within its category (for example, 6 → 1) and tap Move. You can still use the arrows. Changes save to Supabase automatically.

🖼️ Download All Product Photos

Download all product photos from the loaded catalogue into one ZIP file on this device. Works on computers and phones. The ZIP includes a manifest.json linking image filenames to product names and categories.
After downloading, upload products.json to the whitepack-clients repository and replace the old file. Your customer catalogue will then use the updated products.
